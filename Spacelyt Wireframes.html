A single accent line travels from the hero through every section, anchored to the page's centerline. Each section is a "node" the line passes through. Closest to your brief, easiest to build, calmest to read.

Option D · editorial · most "studio portfolio"

Editorial — magazine-style modular blocks

Treats the homepage like a printed monograph. Big mast, table of contents, asymmetric spreads, generous numbering. Motion is subtler (focus is typography and rhythm) but feels expensive and considered. Works well if portfolio imagery will carry weight.

Notes for next round
  • If you want the cleanest, fastest-to-ship homepage → A · Center Spine
  • If you want the most "architecture studio" expression → B · Site Plan
